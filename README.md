## Project Overview
Sweet Delight Cakes is a start-up, home-based bakery established in 2026 with the aim of providing customers with fresh, delicious, affordable, and beautifully designed cakes for their special occasions.

The business started as a small home bakery and was created from a passion for baking, creativity, and bringing happiness to people through cakes. The idea behind Sweet Delight Cakes was inspired by the growing demand for personalised and affordable cakes for occasions such as birthdays, weddings, graduations, baby showers, anniversaries, and other celebrations.

Many customers want cakes that are not only delicious but also match the theme and style of their special event. Sweet Delight Cakes was established to meet this need by offering customers the opportunity to choose their preferred flavours, designs, sizes, and themes.

The business operates as a home-based bakery, allowing Sweet Delight Cakes to focus on producing cakes in small quantities while maintaining attention to quality and detail. Quality ingredients are used to ensure that customers receive fresh and delicious products. Customers can also communicate their ideas and requirements so that cakes can be customised according to their preferences.

Since its establishment, Sweet Delight Cakes has focused on providing friendly customer service, affordable prices, creative designs, and convenient ordering. The business plans to develop its online presence so that customers can easily view available products, learn more about the services offered, and place orders online.

In the future, Sweet Delight Cakes aims to grow from a small home-based bakery into a recognised local cake business. The business hopes to expand its product range, reach more customers, improve its online ordering services, and eventually establish a dedicated bakery location.

## Website Goals and objectives

The purpose of the website is to provide an online platform where customers can easily learn about Sweet Delight Cakes, browse the available products and services, and place cake orders.

**Website Goals:**

* Increase the online visibility of Sweet Delight Cakes.
* Increase website traffic and attract more potential customers.
* Generate more customer enquiries.
* Allow customers to place cake orders online.
* Integrate social media platforms to promote the business and connect with customers.
* Build customer trust through customer testimonials and a gallery of completed cake designs.

**Key Performance Indicators (KPIs):**

| Goal                           | KPI                                         |
| ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Increase website traffic       | Achieve 1,000 website visitors per month    |
| Generate customer enquiries    | Receive at least 50 enquiries per month     |
| Increase online cake orders    | Receive at least 25 online orders per month |
| Improve customer satisfaction  | Achieve 90% positive customer reviews       |
| Increase social media presence | Reach 500 followers within six months       |

### KEY FEATURES AND FUNCTIONALLY

The website will include the following key pages and features:

* **Homepage:** Provides a welcoming introduction to Sweet Delight Cakes and displays featured cakes, current promotions, and customer testimonials.

* **About Us:** Provides information about the history of Sweet Delight Cakes, as well as the business mission, vision, and values.

* **Products and Services:** Displays the different cakes and services offered, including birthday cakes, wedding cakes, graduation cakes, baby shower cakes, cupcakes, and customised cake designs.

* **Gallery:** Displays high-quality photographs of completed cakes and designs to give customers ideas and inspiration.

* **Contact:** Provides customers with a contact form, phone number, email address, business location, and Google Maps integration.

* **Online Ordering:** Allows customers to provide details such as their preferred cake type, flavour, design inspiration, collection or delivery preference, and secure online payment options.

* **Additional Features:** The website will also include a search function, customer reviews, frequently asked questions (FAQ), social media integration, and a newsletter subscription.



#### Timeline and Milestone

The development of the Sweet Delight Cakes website will be completed over an eight-week period. Each week will focus on specific activities and milestones to ensure that the project is completed successfully and that the final website meets the business requirements.

**Week 1 – Research and Requirements:** During the first week, research will be conducted on bakery websites to understand how similar businesses present their products and services online. The target audience will be identified, competitors will be analysed, and the business requirements will be gathered. Information about Sweet Delight Cakes, including its mission, vision, products, and services, will also be collected. The milestone for this week is to complete the research and document the business requirements.

**Week 2 – Planning and Design:** During the second week, the structure of the website will be planned. A sitemap will be created to show how the different pages will be organised and connected. The website pages will be decided on, and the colour scheme, fonts, and branding will be selected to create a suitable cake-themed design. Low-fidelity wireframes will also be developed to provide an initial visual representation of the website layout. The milestone for this week is to complete the website structure and initial design plan.

**Week 3 – HTML Development:** During the third week, the main website pages will be developed using HTML. These pages will include the Homepage, About Us, Products and Services, Gallery, and Contact pages. The content and basic structure of each page will be added according to the requirements identified during the research and planning stages. The milestone for this week is to have the main website pages created using HTML.

**Week 4 – CSS and Visual Design:** During the fourth week, CSS will be applied to improve the appearance and presentation of the website. This will include styling the colours, fonts, layouts, images, navigation menus, buttons, and other cake-themed design elements. The aim will be to create a consistent and visually appealing design throughout the website. The milestone for this week is to complete the website's styling and visual design.

**Week 5 – JavaScript and Functionality:** During the fifth week, JavaScript will be added to provide interactive functionality to the website. This may include interactive elements, form validation, navigation features, and other required website functions. The milestone for this week is to have the required website functionality implemented.

**Week 6 – Testing and Quality Assurance:** During the sixth week, the website will be tested on different devices and screen sizes to ensure that it works correctly and is responsive. Links, forms, navigation, and other website functions will be checked. Any errors or technical and design problems identified during testing will be corrected. The milestone for this week is to complete website testing and resolve the identified issues.

**Week 7 – Documentation and Improvements:** During the seventh week, the project documentation will be completed and the website content will be reviewed. Any necessary improvements to the design, content, and functionality will be made. The project files and documentation will also be checked to ensure that they are properly organised. The milestone for this week is to complete the documentation and final improvements.

**Week 8 – Final Review and Submission:** During the final week, a complete review of the website will be conducted to ensure that all project requirements have been met. The website will be checked for any remaining errors, and the project files will be organised in preparation for submission. The final website and documentation will then be prepared for submission. The milestone for this week is to have the completed website reviewed, organised, and ready for submission.
