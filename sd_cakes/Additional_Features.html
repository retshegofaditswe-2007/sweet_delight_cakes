Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How many days before should I place my order?

We recommend placing your order at least 3–5 days in advance.

Do you make custom cakes?

Yes. We create customised cakes for birthdays, weddings, graduations, and other special occasions.

Do you offer delivery?

Yes. Customers can choose collection or delivery. Delivery fees depend on the delivery location.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept cash and EFT. Please contact us to confirm the available payment options for your order.
